Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our custom medal and pin ordering workflow.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our minimum order quantity is 50 pieces for custom die-cast medals and trading lapel pins. For large tournament orders of 500 to 1,000+ pieces, significant volume discounts apply.

How long does production and delivery take?

Standard turnaround is typically 3 to 4 weeks following digital proof approval. If you are on a tight schedule, contact John directly to discuss rush production availability.

Are there separate mold or design setup fees?

No. We provide free in-house digital artwork proofs and clear, all-inclusive pricing with zero unexpected mold setup surcharges.

What artwork format do I need to provide?

Vector artwork (AI, EPS, PDF, or SVG) is ideal, but high-resolution PNG, JPG, or even a hand-drawn sketch of your mascot or tournament emblem works perfectly. Our artists handle the rest.
