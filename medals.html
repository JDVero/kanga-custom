Material & Mold Sculpting

High-pressure die-cast zinc alloy metal. Available in 2D multi-level relief or full 3D sculpted contour molds with custom cutouts and intricate detailing.

Plating & Enamel Finishes

Plating choices include Antique Gold, Antique Silver, Antique Bronze, Shiny Gold, Shiny Nickel, and Black Nickel. Translucent or solid Pantone-matched enamel fills.

03

Custom Sublimated Ribbons

Full-color dye-sublimated satin neck ribbons (typically 30" to 36" length, 1.0" to 1.5" width) sewn directly to the medal loop with customized tournament branding.
