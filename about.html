The Stress-Free 4-Step Process

From your first phone call to finals day medal ceremonies, we guide every milestone.

STEP 01

Direct Consultation

Call or email us with your tournament date, estimated team numbers, and medal size preferences.

STEP 02

3D Digital Proofing

Our artist drafts a full-color digital proof with exact Pantone enamel colors and custom ribbon artwork.

STEP 03

Precision Production

Medals are die-cast from solid zinc alloy, hand-enameled, polished, and assembled with ribbons.

STEP 04

On-Time Delivery

Carefully boxed and delivered directly to your venue or club headquarters before finals weekend.
