About Kanga Custom Products

Founded in South Salt Lake City, we are dedicated to elevating tournament experiences with premium, custom-crafted awards.

Our Heritage

Crafting Memorable Finals Day Moments Since Day One

Kanga Custom Products was founded in South Salt Lake City, Utah, by owner John Bouwer with a singular mission: to eliminate unnecessary distributor markups and deliver factory-direct, heavyweight custom sporting awards to local leagues, regional tournaments, and scholastic teams.

For over a decade, we have partnered directly with tournament directors, club presidents, and coaches across soccer, baseball, softball, basketball, swimming, and martial arts. We understand that on tournament finals day, the medals and pins placed around young athletes' necks represent months of grit, teamwork, and dedication.

“

When young athletes step onto the podium on finals day, their medal is a lifelong keepsake of achievement. Our job is to make sure every single piece feels heavyweight, looks magnificent, and arrives on time—every time.

John Bouwer Owner & Managing Director, Kanga Custom Products

What Sets Kanga Apart

Unlike online catalogue aggregators that drop-ship generic stock blanks, Kanga Custom Products operates as a direct overseas manufacturing partner. Every custom medal, pin, and sublimated ribbon is precision-engineered to your exact tournament logo, color palette, and event branding with zero setup fees on qualifying volume orders.
